HabitProof policies

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. About this notice

HabitProof helps people build habits and lets participating schools or administrators assign activities and review proof of completion. This notice explains how information is handled when you use the app.

2. Information we handle

When you create an account, we handle your name, email address, account credentials (managed through Firebase Authentication), account role, and account or school identifiers. The app also stores activities, schedules, completion status, and related habit statistics. If you submit proof, the photos you choose to capture and upload and their submission timestamps are stored with that submission. Administrators may provide school details and a logo.

3. How information is used

We use this information to create and secure accounts, provide habit tracking, deliver reminders, receive and display submissions, support school review, calculate progress summaries, and maintain and troubleshoot the service. The app may collect diagnostic, performance, and usage events to help operate and improve its features.

4. Who can see information

Your submitted proof and activity information may be visible to the school administrators associated with your account, so they can manage activities and review submissions. Information is processed by service providers used to operate the app, including Google Firebase services for authentication, database, file storage, and diagnostics. We do not sell personal information.

5. Photos, permissions, and retention

Camera access is requested when you choose to capture proof. Photos are uploaded only when you submit them. Notifications are used for activity reminders and can be controlled in your device settings. Uploaded information is retained as needed to provide the service, support school review, and meet operational or legal requirements; exact retention may depend on the school or service configuration. Removing the app does not itself delete information already uploaded.

6. Your choices and account questions

You can choose whether to submit photo proof and can manage notification permission in your device settings. You can delete your account on the HabitProof website or from your in-app profile. Deletion removes your account profile, personal activity data, submissions, and proof photos. School-managed records may need to be retained by the school. If an administrator deletes their account, the school and student records remain, while that admin association and school join codes are removed.

7. Children and schools

Where HabitProof is used by students, the school or account administrator is responsible for ensuring that use and any required permissions are appropriate for the students it invites. Please do not create an account unless you are permitted to do so by your school or guardian where applicable.

8. Changes

We may update this notice as the service changes. The current version is available in the app. Continued use after an update means the revised notice applies to future use.
